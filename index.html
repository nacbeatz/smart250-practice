Lionel Messi yongereye amasezerano muri Inter Miami

Rutahizamu w’Umunya-Argentine, Lionel Messi, yongereye amasezerano azatuma ageza imyaka 41 akiri umukinnyi wa Inter Miami yo muri Leta Zunze Ubumwe za Amerika. Messi yasinye indi myaka itatu izarangira mu 2028 nk’uko byatangajwe n’ikipe ya Inter Miami. Uyu mukinnyi yatsinze ibitego 71 mu mikino 82, atanga indi mipira 37 yavuyemo ibitego, kuva aje muri Inter Miami mu 2023 avuye muri Paris Saint-Germain yo mu Bufaransa.
