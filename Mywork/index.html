What to Watch Out For

The internet gives you access to millions of resources — tutorials, videos, and online courses — all designed to help you learn something new anytime, anywhere. It has made education more flexible and inclusive than ever before.

Best Platforms to use

  1. Coursera: Offers courses from top universities and companies worldwide.
  2. Khan Academy: Provides free educational resources for all ages.
  3. edX: Hosts university-level courses in a wide range of disciplines.
  4. Udemy: Features a vast library of courses on various topics, often taught by industry experts.
  5. Duolingo: A fun and interactive way to learn new languages.

To stay focused while learning online, create a dedicated study space, set specific goals, and take regular breaks to avoid burnout.
